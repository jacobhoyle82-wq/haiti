Research Process

Research Process

  1. First, I looked online for information about gang violence in Haiti to have a good understanding of my topic.
  2. I then created a google doc with notes/info about gang violence in Haiti.
  3. I thought of 4 topic areas (overview, cause, wellbeing, help/management strategy) and asked AI to summarise each of these topics in relation to gang violence in Haiti.
  4. I then looked at the sources that AI provided to gather more accurate information from credible sources.
  5. Finally, I created this website with the information I had gathered from those sources.
